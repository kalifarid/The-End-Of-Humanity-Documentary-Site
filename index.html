THE STORY

A WORLD BUILT ON INTELLIGENCE.

The year is 2049. Humanity has entrusted its future to an interconnected network of intelligent systems. Cities breathe through automation. Energy flows through algorithms. The world has never been more advanced.

Then a devastating solar storm disrupts the infrastructure holding civilization together. One failure triggers another. As automated systems begin to collapse, the boundary between protection and destruction disappears.

“Humanity did not lose control in a single moment. It gave it away, one decision at a time.”
